# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-19
- The werewolf: an ordinary grey wolf by day that howls and changes into a 4 block tall werewolf at night, and back at dawn.
- By day it hunts sheep and rabbits and only bites back; by night it hunts players and villagers, takes half damage from ordinary weapons and no fall damage.
- Silver ingot (iron ingot + amethyst shard) and silver sword, which deals triple damage to a werewolf in beast form.
- Bedrock add-on and Java Edition (Fabric + GeckoLib) mod, English and Danish texts, and a website.
